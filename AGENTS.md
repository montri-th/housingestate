# CityMETER Housing Estate — owner preferences

Set 2026-11-18 (release 0.1.0) on the owner request, VERBATIM: “จากที่เราทำ demo ของ CityMETER: University มาแล้ว ใน https://github.com/montri-th/university ขอให้ทำแบบเดียวกันให้กับ CityMETER: Housing estate แล้ว publish ที่ https://github.com/montri-th/housingestate · ดูเรื่อง ขอบเขต campus ให้แสดงผลในลักษณะเดียวกับ University นะ · โดยใช้ข้อมูลจาก [the housing estate research master] ซึ่งมีทั้งข้อมูล master ใน repo และโครงการใหม่ที่ research เพิ่ม ให้แสดงบน web demo ให้มากที่สุด · update dataset registry sheet ด้วยนะ และพี่ช่วยสร้าง repo ใหม่ใน github ให้ด้วย”. “แบบเดียวกัน” = the CityMETER · University contract (montri-th/university @f610edb, release 0.1.5, citymeter-shell 0.6.2), itself the Hotel contract through School, so every University, School or Hotel rule that is not about universities, schools or hotels applies here as written. University-only features (students, reporting units, faculties and their labels, fees, quality, logos, the 2566 roster) have no counterpart here and are not carried.

- Apply current LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1, color-srgb-10) and the Location Intelligence Profile for LDS 0.9.7 (`li.supply` for allocated plots), and preserve the map-first CityMETER layout. Colours come only from `assets/theme.css`, `js/scales.js` and `js/cues.js`; never approximate a value by eye. Analytical colours are the same HEX on both themes; EVID-05 map treatments use the `dataState` tokens. Colour scales always use the full 41 LDS samples (owner standing rule “Color scale ขอให้ใช้ 41 scales ตาม LDS เสมอ”). `npm run check:lds -- --lds <base> --li <profile>` compares every shipped value with both source files.
- Read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues. The question is “แปลงจัดสรรกระจุกอยู่ที่ไหน และรอบแปลงมีหมู่บ้านแบบไหน”.
- Two grains, never mixed (`DATA_METHOD.md` §2). **Records** (the 19,753 DOL website allocation records of the CityMETER production snapshot) carry the plots and the production district and province they are counted in; they are the measure. A record is a registration, never a village; records are never merged. **Projects** (the 1,538 entries of developers' own catalogues the research team collected) carry the pins, the extents, the labels, prices, launches and evidence; a project carries no plots of its own. A project and a record are paired only as the research team paired them, each pair named by how it was found (`meta.pairs`); held pairs stay out.
- Measure is a contract (`js/model.js#METRICS`): allocated plots (Σ the selected plot totals of the records, “แปลง”, `li.supply`) by default, records (“รายการ”, `count`) on the switch; the map, legend, labels, Aha, ranks and charts read the same measure. The plot-type filter narrows the measure to that type's plots and the records reporting it. Never call a figure villages, finished houses, households, sales, a sales status, demand, a market size, a density or a ranking of quality.
- Areas: the province and the district production gives each record (its query boundary, not an address); never re-assign it by a point or a name at runtime. The 77 production provinces are drawn and filled; with a province chosen, its districts carry the measure as chips at their label points (the swatch a 41-sample ramp over that province's districts). A province or district without a record under the filters is measured zero, never “no estate”.
- House types are the seven ids of `meta.houseTypes`, bound by stable id to LDS series with their shape cue (`js/cues.js#GROUP_SERIES`; no star). Plot types (บ้านเดี่ยว · บ้านแฝด · บ้านแถว · อาคารพาณิชย์ · ที่ดินเปล่า · อื่น ๆ) are parts of the measure, drawn as neutral bars (`--border-emphasis`) — never given the house types' series, the two vocabularies are different. The 41-sample ramp colours the province fills, the province chart and the district chips' swatches only.
- Aha first: on the whole country, the provinces with the most of the measure (at most three; never cut through a tie, never every province that holds the measure; `js/area-insight.js#topWithoutTie`), their share of the national total with numerator and denominator, the same share among house plots only as a second bar (both bases in each bar's title and accessible text) and the provinces with no record by name; in a province, the districts with the most of the measure (at most three) and their share of the province. Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, never UI constants.
- The search is a finder (projects, developers, districts, provinces and records by name, other names or allocator): it narrows the project pins and the table and lists what it found above the overview; it never narrows the measure.
- Pins: the reference pin the research team selected from the publisher's own evidence (`27_MAP_REFERENCES`, the default layer). Coordinates from Google Maps never enter the data: a pin whose source is a Google Maps link stays off the map (`meta.counts.pinsOffMap`). Production's district centroids are never drawn as projects.
- Labels, launches and prices are what the source showed when the research team read it: a label is never a sales status, a price is never compared, averaged or ranked, a launch is never read from a permit, news or inspection date.
- Legal roles are shown as the notice, brochure, EIA record or company report gives them, with the source, never as a land register. An allocator is named only when its name carries a legal-entity form; the names of natural persons are never published.
- Estate extents are OpenStreetMap areas matched to the pin by place and name (`scripts/match-extents.py`; ODbL, © OpenStreetMap contributors), approximate: “กรอบราว N ไร่” (m² / 1,600), never a deed, a legal boundary or a land size.
- Buildings lit with an open project (the University rule of 2026-11-12, owner 2026-11-18: “ดูเรื่อง ขอบเขต campus ให้แสดงผลในลักษณะเดียวกับ University นะ”): every drawn building in the project's extent (`js/extent-buildings.js`: the interior point of one of its pieces inside the extent, outside its holes; the building lit whole) in `map.activeLayer`; the legend says “อาคารในกรอบโครงการ”. A lit building opens the two-point building camera (“อาคารในกรอบโครงการ”, “สูงราว”); it carries no figure. Never a count of lit buildings and never the whole focus the join was given. `js/massing.js` tests membership with `match` (hash), not `in` on a literal list.
- Context comes from datasets of the same registry, joined by id with the commit and SHA-256 recorded (`meta.context`): CityMETER · Transit stations (operating stations drawn as quiet clickable rings, 1 km round a station; under construction named in words only). Every distance is a straight line between reference points — never a walking route, a travel time or a catchment.
- Focus + quiet context: detail (fills, district chips, coloured cue symbols, extents, lit buildings) only for the focus; everything else stays visible but quiet. The optional files load after the first paint, with or without a map — `data/extents.json` (1.2 s), `data/portfolio.json` (2.6 s), `data/records.json` (4 s) — or at once when a view needs them; until then the view says it is loading, never empty, and a failure is said by `PARTIAL`.
- Names near the middle of the view (`js/labels.js`): project labels in the central ellipse, at most 9 on desktop and 5 on a phone; a label is the house-type cue and a short name (a leading โครงการ/หมู่บ้าน dropped); hover or focus opens a card of icon chips.
- Buildings in detail: a project's focus is its extent and its surroundings (`js/regions.js`: 800 m past the extent, at least 1,000 m), and wherever the reader moves the camera away from the subject a 3 km square round the camera joins from zoom 12. `data/building-units/` is the University set unchanged (the Hotel units plus the อปท. round the campuses, by the 1.15 × rule); 726 of the 798 pins stand in an evaluated unit. Extending the units round the remaining estates uses `scripts/build-campus-units.py` with the estates' squares.
- Terrain and the building view (`js/shell/terrain.js`, `js/shell/camera.js`, `js/massing.js`, `js/shell/venue-xray.js`, `js/shell/maplibre-guard.js`, MapLibre pinned at 5.6.2, `GEOJSON_MAXZOOM`) follow the University, Hotel and School contracts unchanged. A project opens in the `model` preset framed on its extent; a building of its extent opens the two-point camera. Do not reorder the massing, x-ray, station, extent and pin layers casually.
- Breadcrumb is ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › เขต/อำเภอ › the project, with production codes and the production estate layer (`?d=estate`) on every administrative crumb; the project crumb has no production link.
- Every panel view ends with exactly one provenance line (`provenance()` in `js/panel.js`): dataset · source · date · boundary as pictogram-led parts, then “วิธีนับ” (DATA_METHOD.md); the developers' lists, companies' notices and reports, OpenStreetMap, rail and terrain join the source part when the view shows them. No caveat anywhere on screen (Hotel owner rule of 2026-11-01, “ไม่มี caveats เลย”): exactness comes from the names, the numerator and denominator of every share and the provenance line (`tests/views.test.js`).
- Copy: short, natural Thai in the reader's words — แปลงจัดสรร / รายการ / โครงการ / ผู้พัฒนา / ผู้จัดสรร / ประเภทแปลง / ประเภทบ้าน / เขต·อำเภอ / จังหวัด / สถานี / รอบแปลง; pictograms from the approved subset carry what a sentence used to; sources, methods and ids behind one “ที่มาและพิกัด” disclosure; units on every number; a number and its unit never split across lines. No static counts in page copy (help, meta): numbers come from the data.
- English (`?lang=en`, citymeter-shell i18n): `js/lang.js` registers `js/lang-en.js` and the English the data carries (province and district names of the area index, station nameEn, and the plot types, house types, scopes, labels, pin roles, pair levels, legal roles and event classes the data files carry). Project, brand, Thai-named publisher, allocator and DOL record names have no English in the data and stay Thai. Years are ค.ศ. in English. Run a harvest in English (every view, `window.__citymeterI18n.missing` empty) before a release.
- Outbound actions (`js/ask.js`): ถาม Google AI with a question written for the view, Google Maps search, Street View only where the view has a point. Public names and published points only. Nothing from Google's answers enters the data.
- Phone layout (Hotel 0.3.5, School 0.6.0, University 0.1.4): below 761 px in portrait the Aha line and the legend are one dock and the sheet has three heights (34 % for a project, building, station, district or record; 42 % for the overview); a project opens in the `model` preset above the sheet; verify at 390 px in light and dark.
- Privacy and publication: never copy review queues, identity or scope-review statuses, review groups, issue flags, registration numbers, researcher notes, raw source locators, the raw JSON of the DOL pages, event ids or internal Sheet / Drive URLs (`meta.excludedFields`); source links are public https pages only. Never commit the research sheet, its cells, its export or its address, the dataset registry's address, credentials, the full LDS library or the Location Intelligence Profile (`tests/release.test.js` knows the sheet addresses only by their SHA-256). Never put Landometer prices, contracts, proposals or pipeline in this repository. Review Thai and English at desktop and 390 px in light and dark before claiming done; local renders are not proof of a live release — run `npm run verify:live` and walk the live site.
- Releases bump one cache tag `?v=YYYYMMDD-rN` on every entry in `index.html` and, through the import map there, on every ES module, together with `package.json` version, `design-intent.json` and the handoff JSON `productVersion` (`tests/release.test.js`); the optional data files take the same tag from `import.meta.url`. A new module must be added to the import map. Icon fonts inside the stylesheets carry `?v=<first 8 hex of their SHA-256>` (`tests/icons.test.js`).
- Registry: the CityMETER Dataset Registry sheet row `research.TH.housing_estate` carries the demo columns (web, repository, checked date, check note); edit those cells in place, never a new copy of the sheet.

## Shared shell (citymeter-shell)

- `js/shell/` is citymeter-shell, the same bytes in every CityMETER demo; `js/shell/manifest.json` names the version and the hash of every file (`tests/shell.test.js`). Never edit it in one repository alone.
- This release ships **citymeter-shell 0.6.3**: 0.6.2 (Commercial District in the picker, 2026-11-16) plus Housing Estate in the dataset picker (`js/shell/datasets.js`: `{path: 'housingestate', name: 'Housing Estate', group: 'Land', at: true, en: true}`). A demo still on 0.6.2 differs from this repository in `datasets.js` and `manifest.json` only; it lists Housing Estate once it takes 0.6.3 (the family sync: every demo, the hub and the Pages deploy in one batch, owner approval once).
- Everything else in the shell is as School 0.6.0 describes it: the header and dataset picker, the address grammar (`?d=estate&at=<production path>&e=<kind>:<key>&n=<kind>:<key>` with `m`, `f.<filter>`, `focus`, `view`, `v3d`; this demo opens provinces and districts (`at=BKK/D/1012`), its `e` kinds are `site`, `record` and `station`, its `n` kind is `company`, provisional until the registry declares them), the breadcrumb, the tab title within 60 characters, the launch card that sets the theme before the first paint, the partial-result notice (`PARTIAL` in `js/app.js`: every optional file has an entry and its failure calls `partial(key)`), the map budget by device, the lost-context restart, the phone sheet, and the map modules (camera, terrain, x-ray, building join, building units, MVT, map style, MapLibre guard, vendors).
- On citymeter.pages.dev the modules are also served from `/…/r/<digest>/` with a one-year cache: never give a module an address relative to the page, keep data fetches relative to the page (`data/…`). That site sends a Content-Security-Policy: a new outside host must be added there first.
